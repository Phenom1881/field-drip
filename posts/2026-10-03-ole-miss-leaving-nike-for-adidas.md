Ole Miss is on its way out of Nike. On3's Brett McMurphy and Pete Nakos reported on Friday that the Rebels are finalizing an agreement to make Adidas the official apparel partner for Ole Miss athletics, starting when the current Nike contract runs out in June 2027. Chancellor Glenn Boyce and athletics director Keith Carter put the deal together.

The Rebels have worn Nike since 1999. Their last extension, announced in 2015, ran 12 years and covers every school year through 2026-27.

## What has been reported

Nothing is official yet. Neither the school nor Adidas has announced the deal, and the outlets covering it disagree on the money:

- Yahoo Sports describes a seven-year agreement worth $140 million, running from July 1, 2027 through 2034.
- Saturday Down South, citing The Ole Miss Spirit, reports a 10-year deal worth $200 million to $300 million.
- Red Cup Rebellion puts the value at $20 million or more a year.

Those numbers can't all be right, which is why the deal sits at **Reported** on [our status ladder](~/about/#status). It moves to **Official** when the school or Adidas announces it. Ole Miss is a public university, so the signed contract should be available through a public records request, though parts of it can be redacted.

Reports also say the deal is expected to include support for athletes' name, image, and likeness opportunities, and that Adidas will work with the Rebels on branding and future uniform designs.

## Half of a trade

The timing lines up with a move in the other direction. About two weeks earlier, [Miami](~/schools/miami/) agreed to return to Nike on a 10-year deal reported at more than $200 million, after Adidas's counteroffer fell short. Miami's Adidas contract also expires in June 2027, so on July 1, 2027, the two programs effectively trade brands.

It's part of a busy year for Adidas, which took over at [Penn State](~/schools/penn-state/) and [Tennessee](~/schools/tennessee/) on July 1 and has since announced deals with [Utah](~/schools/utah/), starting in 2027, and [Colorado State](~/schools/colorado-state/).

## What to watch

- An official announcement from Ole Miss or Adidas, with confirmed length and value.
- Whether the final terms land closer to the seven-year or the 10-year reports.
- Uniform reveals. Tennessee showed its new Adidas looks the week after its deal took effect, so expect something similar in the summer of 2027.
