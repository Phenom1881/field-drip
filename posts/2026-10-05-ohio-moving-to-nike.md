Ohio is switching to Nike. On Friday, Ohio Athletics announced a five-year agreement with Nike and BSN SPORTS to make Nike the Bobcats' official apparel, uniform, equipment, and accessories provider. Nike will outfit all 16 Ohio programs starting in July 2027.

This one is official. The school announced it, so it sits at **Official** on [our status ladder](~/about/#status) and moves to **In effect** when the deal starts. Ohio did not release the value or an exact end date.

## Ten years of Adidas

Ohio moved to Adidas on July 1, 2017, on an eight-year deal that replaced Russell Athletic, which had outfitted Bobcat teams since 2007. In July 2025, the school extended with Adidas through June 30, 2027 and brought in Game One as its team dealer. That extension turned out to be a bridge. Nike takes over the day after it ends.

## How the deal works

Ohio will get its Nike gear through BSN SPORTS, the same team dealer behind recent Nike deals at [Hawaii](~/schools/hawaii/) and [Western Kentucky](~/schools/wku/). BSN handles distribution and manages the partnership.

"Ohio Athletics is thrilled to enter into a new agreement with Nike," athletics director Slade Larscheid said in the announcement. "Nike is the industry leader in sports apparel, performance, style, and innovation. Additionally, the level of service and engagement with BSN SPORTS is a game-changer for our teams."

## Where it leaves the MAC

Based on the deals on file today, Ohio's move makes Nike the majority brand in the MAC. Nike already outfits [Akron](~/schools/akron/), [Ball State](~/schools/ball-state/), [Bowling Green](~/schools/bowling-green/), [Buffalo](~/schools/buffalo/), [Sacramento State](~/schools/sacramento-state/), and [Toledo](~/schools/toledo/), so Ohio would be the seventh of 13 football members. Adidas would keep [Central Michigan](~/schools/central-michigan/), [Eastern Michigan](~/schools/eastern-michigan/), [Miami (OH)](~/schools/miami-oh/), [UMass](~/schools/umass/), and [Western Michigan](~/schools/western-michigan/), and Under Armour has [Kent State](~/schools/kent-state/). Several of those deals could change before 2027.

## What to watch

- An end date and value, which could come out through a public records request since Ohio is a public university.
- Uniform reveals, most likely ahead of the 2027 season.
