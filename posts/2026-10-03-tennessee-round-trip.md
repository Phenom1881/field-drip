Tennessee has now worn Adidas, then Nike, then Adidas again, all inside about 30 years.

## The first Adidas era

Tennessee's Adidas relationship began in 1995. The Vols wore the three stripes through their dominant run in the 1990s, including the 1998 national championship, which is still the program's most recent football title.

## Eleven years of Nike

In January 2014, Tennessee announced a department-wide agreement with Nike. The Adidas contract ran through June 30, 2015, so Nike gear arrived with the 2015 football season. CBS Sports reported the original deal at eight years and nearly $4 million a year in cash and merchandise credit, plus a $2 million signing bonus. A later amendment carried the Nike contract through June 30, 2026.

## Why Tennessee went back

As that end date approached, Tennessee and Nike couldn't agree on an extension. In August 2025, the athletics department announced it would reunite with Adidas on a 10-year agreement, effective July 1, 2026. Reports have valued the deal at about $20 million a year, and it includes investment in athletes' name, image, and likeness opportunities.

The deal took effect on July 1. Adidas marked the launch with a video narrated by Peyton Manning, who also modeled the new home jersey, and new Adidas gear was set to hit stores on July 10.

## The pattern

Tennessee's return is part of a bigger Adidas push. [Penn State](~/schools/penn-state/) made the same move away from Nike on the same day, and [Ole Miss](~/schools/ole-miss/) is reported to be next, in 2027.
