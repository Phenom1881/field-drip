Northwestern is staying with Under Armour. On Thursday, October 8, the school announced a 10-year extension that keeps Under Armour as the Wildcats' apparel and equipment partner through at least 2037. The current contract was set to expire in 2027.

The school announced it, so the extension is **Official** on [our status ladder](~/about/#status), and the current deal stays **In effect**. Northwestern did not release a dollar value.

## What's in the deal

According to [Northwestern's announcement](https://nusports.com/news/2026/10/8/northwestern-athletics-and-under-armour-announce-landmark-10-year-partnership-extension-through-2037), the extension covers:

- Footwear, apparel, and equipment for all 21 varsity programs, with larger allocations than before
- Specialty and alternate uniforms designed with athletes for big games
- New NIL programs that let athletes work with the brand and its design teams
- More annual marketing support from Under Armour, plus a larger retail presence in Chicago and nationally

"Under Armour is an extraordinary partner for Northwestern Athletics," said Mark Jackson, Northwestern's vice president for athletics and recreation. [The Daily Northwestern](https://dailynorthwestern.com/2026/10/09/lateststories/northwestern-extends-apparel-partnership-with-under-armour-through-2037) reported that Jackson said the new terms "reflect a shared ambition to compete and win at the highest level."

## Under Armour since 2012

Northwestern signed with Under Armour in 2011 and started wearing the brand in 2012, becoming Under Armour's first Big Ten school. The two sides extended in March 2025, but that release gave no term. Last week's announcement fills that gap: the old deal ran to 2027, and the new one runs a decade past it.

## Where Under Armour stands in the Big Ten

Based on the deals on file today, Under Armour has three of the Big Ten's 18 football programs: Northwestern, [Maryland](~/schools/maryland/) (through 2036), and [Wisconsin](~/schools/wisconsin/) (through 2033, with an option through 2036). Nike has 11 counting Jordan Brand at Michigan and UCLA, and Adidas has four.

All three Under Armour schools have now extended for at least a decade. Wisconsin's extension last November carried a reported minimum of $104.5 million over 10 years. Northwestern's value hasn't been reported.

The renewal comes in a mixed stretch for Under Armour elsewhere. It lost [South Carolina](~/schools/south-carolina/) to Nike this summer and will lose [Utah](~/schools/utah/) to Adidas in July 2027, but it added [Georgia Tech](~/schools/georgia-tech/) from Adidas this year.
