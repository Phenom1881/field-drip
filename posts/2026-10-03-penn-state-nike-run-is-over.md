For 33 years, Penn State and Nike looked like a permanent pairing. The partnership started in 1993, the same year the Nittany Lions joined the Big Ten, and the relationship between Nike founder Phil Knight and coach Joe Paterno helped hold it together. Before Nike, Penn State wore Russell Athletic.

## How it ended

The first sign came in September 2025, when On3's Brett McMurphy reported that Penn State was close to a switch to Adidas, pending approval from its Board of Trustees. The athletics department said at the time that it was running a request for proposals for its next apparel partner.

Penn State then announced a 10-year agreement with Adidas through a video captioned with its start date, July 1, 2026, written in Roman numerals. Bleacher Report has put the value at roughly $300 million. Adidas also promised a broader line of Penn State gear for fans.

## Where it leaves the Big Ten

Penn State joined [Indiana](~/schools/indiana/), [Nebraska](~/schools/nebraska/), and [Washington](~/schools/washington/) as the Big Ten's Adidas schools. [Rutgers](~/schools/rutgers/), which had been with Adidas, moved to Nike in July 2025. The Nittany Lions opened the 2026 season in their new Adidas uniforms.

## The bigger picture

Adidas has been pushing to land big brands, often by outbidding Nike, and it paid off twice on July 1, 2026, when Penn State and [Tennessee](~/schools/tennessee/) both changed over. With [Ole Miss](~/schools/ole-miss/) reported to follow in 2027, the three stripes are building a real presence in the SEC and the Big Ten.
