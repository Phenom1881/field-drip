UMass is switching to Under Armour. On Saturday, October 10, Under Armour announced an eight-year partnership that makes it the official outfitter of Massachusetts Athletics starting July 1, 2027. Under Armour will supply uniforms, apparel, footwear, accessories, and equipment for all 21 UMass varsity programs, plus coaches and staff.

The brand announced it, so the new deal is **Official** on [our status ladder](~/about/#status). It moves to **In effect** when it starts. The current Adidas deal stays **In effect** until then. Neither side released a dollar value or an exact end date.

## What's in the deal

According to [Under Armour's announcement](https://about.underarmour.com/en/stories/2026/10/massachusetts-athletics-and-under-armour-announce-long-term-appa.html), the partnership:

- Runs eight years from July 1, 2027
- Makes Under Armour the exclusive provider of uniforms, apparel, footwear, accessories, and sports equipment for varsity teams, coaches, and staff
- Covers all 21 varsity programs and the athletics staff

"We are thrilled to announce our new partnership with Under Armour," UMass athletics director Ryan Bamford said in the release, calling it an important moment for the department. The announcement points to UMass's move to the Mid-American Conference in 2025 and its best Learfield Directors' Cup finish in 25 years.

## Two decades of Adidas

UMass has worn Adidas since 2005. In October 2018, the school [announced a 10-year extension](https://umassathletics.com/news/2018/10/17/general-university-of-massachusetts-adidas-announce-10-year-partnership-extension) that ran through the 2027-28 season. Under Armour's deal starts a year before that, on July 1, 2027. Neither side has said how the last year of the Adidas deal is being handled, so we now list the Adidas deal as ending June 30, 2027.

## Where it leaves the MAC

Based on the deals on file today, UMass would be the MAC's second Under Armour school, after [Kent State](~/schools/kent-state/). Once UMass and [Ohio](~/schools/ohio/) switch in July 2027, the MAC's 13 football members would split this way:

- **Nike (7):** [Akron](~/schools/akron/), [Ball State](~/schools/ball-state/), [Bowling Green](~/schools/bowling-green/), [Buffalo](~/schools/buffalo/), [Ohio](~/schools/ohio/), [Sacramento State](~/schools/sacramento-state/), and [Toledo](~/schools/toledo/)
- **Adidas (4):** [Central Michigan](~/schools/central-michigan/), [Eastern Michigan](~/schools/eastern-michigan/), [Miami (OH)](~/schools/miami-oh/), and [Western Michigan](~/schools/western-michigan/)
- **Under Armour (2):** Kent State and UMass

Adidas would lose two MAC schools in the same summer.

## A busy week for Under Armour

The UMass deal came two days after [Northwestern](~/schools/northwestern/) extended with Under Armour through 2037. Under Armour added [Georgia Tech](~/schools/georgia-tech/) from Adidas this year, but it lost [South Carolina](~/schools/south-carolina/) to Nike this summer and will lose [Utah](~/schools/utah/) to Adidas in July 2027.

## What to watch

- A dollar value and an end date. UMass is a public university, so the contract could come out through a public records request.
- The first Under Armour uniforms, most likely ahead of the 2027 season.
