Field Drip tracks who outfits college football: which brand each FBS program wears, when each deal ends, and who is switching. Every deal on the site links to the reporting behind it.

Field Drip is independent. It isn't affiliated with any school, conference, or apparel company.

## How we label status {#status}

Every deal sits on one rung of the same ladder.

- **Rumored**: talk from fans, insiders, or social media, with no established outlet reporting it.
- **Reported**: at least one established outlet reports the deal, but the school and the brand haven't announced it.
- **Agreed**: outlets report a finished agreement, but there is still no official announcement.
- **Official**: the school or the brand has announced the deal.
- **In effect**: the deal has started and the program is wearing the new brand.

Finished deals are marked **Ended** and stay on each school's page as history.

## Sources and corrections {#corrections}

Every deal lists its sources. When outlets disagree on length or value, we show each report side by side instead of picking one. Dates and terms are updated when a school or brand confirms them, and each deal shows the date it was last checked.

Spot something wrong? Corrections are welcome, and fixes are noted on the deal itself.
